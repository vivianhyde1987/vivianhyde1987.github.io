# Rose Blog Materials Style Guide

All future generated or edited visual assets must follow this locked direction:

- Transparent background.
- Light-realistic vector illustration feeling.
- Low saturation and warm color temperature.
- Mid-century walnut blog and cabin atmosphere.
- Soft, clean edges without a harsh sticker outline.
- Preserve the real object's identifying shape, texture, proportions, and character.
- Avoid cheap cartoon styling, anime exaggeration, candy colors, busy backgrounds, and visible checkerboards.
- Prefer amber light, walnut brown, sage green, muted blue-green, cream white, and natural tabby tones.
- Assets should work together as one coherent long-term library, not as unrelated standalone images.

Chinese reference: 透明背景、轻写实矢量插画感、低饱和、暖色调、中古胡桃木博客风、柔和边缘、保留真实物体特征。
